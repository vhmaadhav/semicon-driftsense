At the real 200-pair scale the F1 sd is 0.0314, so the shipped mean sits only
0.26 sigma above the gate. The "+4 earned, margin 0.008" framing in issue #30 and
PHASE2_STATE reads the margin off the 2250-pair point estimate; at grading
sample size it is a ~2-in-3 proposition, not a lock. Expected bonus 2.59 pts of
4, i.e. ~1.4 pts of unscored risk. setc moves it to 2.77 (+0.18 expected).

=== verdict ===
setc_last: no regression anywhere, +0.14 total, +0.18 expected bonus. Below the
promotion gate on total, so not a promote by the stated rule -- but it is a
free strict improvement if the gate is read as "no regression". Judgement call.
The real lever is raising F1 itself: every +0.01 F1 is worth ~0.15 direct points
AND buys several points of expected bonus, because of the 4-point cliff at 0.90.

=== ADDENDUM: the fixed-threshold comparison understated setc badly ===
The table above scores both checkpoints at the SAME fixed threshold (0.202).
That is the shipped operating point for driftsense.pt, but it is NOT setc's --
setc's score distribution sits lower (its best-possible F1 is at 0.163). Judging
a checkpoint at another checkpoint's threshold is not a like-for-like test:
whatever ships gets its own threshold fitted anyway.

Re-run with the threshold fitted per checkpoint by 4-fold CV (fit on the
training folds, sweep the threshold on those folds for TOTAL points, score the
held-out fold -- scripts/rejector_cv.py protocol, out-of-sample by construction):

  statistic min(score,zncc)   held-out total     F1
    shipped driftsense.pt          76.89      0.8998
    setc_last                      77.22      0.9156     (+0.33, +0.0158 F1)

Gate probability, fully out-of-fold decisions, stratified 200-pair draws
(A=70/B=70/C=40, N=20000):

              mean F1      sd    P(F1>=0.90)   E[bonus] of 4
  shipped      0.9008  0.0340       52.8%          2.11
  setc         0.9154  0.0310       70.8%          2.83
  paired delta +0.0146   P(setc better) 70.0%   P(tie) 11.2%

EXPECTED VALUE OF PROMOTING setc: +0.33 direct + 0.72 bonus = ~+1.05 pts,
three-quarters of it from de-risking the +4 rather than from the scoreboard.

Direct delta +0.33 is a hair under the +0.35 promotion gate; the bonus term is
what carries the decision. Recommend promoting setc_last AND re-fitting its
threshold (~0.163-0.20 by CV) rather than inheriting 0.202.

NOTE this does NOT contradict the "do not retune the threshold" negative above.
That experiment tuned the threshold to maximise P(F1>=0.90) directly -- a noisy,
non-transferring objective. Fitting it for TOTAL POINTS is smooth and does
transfer; the CV numbers here are out-of-sample by construction. Tune on total,
never on gate probability.
