# Semicon Drift-Sense: evidence-backed novelty and paper-readiness report

## 1. Bottom line

**Unverified — research judgment:** **Go conditionally** with *Geometry- and ambiguity-aware evaluation of synthetic SEM navigation*, centred on A+C and supported by B, after scorer reconciliation, a fresh canvas-disjoint confirmation, and a releasable benchmark or generator-independent replication. **Measured in repo:** current evidence contains useful mechanisms but also stale shipping claims and incompatible scorers, so neither the learned-localiser headline nor Phase 3 saturation is ready to carry a paper. **Unverified — biggest risk:** the findings may remain specific to an unavailable synthetic measurement chain, without a legally releasable artifact or demonstrated transfer to independent SEM data.

## 5. Recommended framing(s) and existing support

**Unverified — rank 1: Geometry- and ambiguity-aware evaluation of synthetic SEM navigation.** Proposed claim: coordinate conventions, observable target definitions, sampling and ambiguity assumptions can change registration conclusions, and an executable audit protocol exposes these effects. A+C carry the contribution; B supplies a mechanistic case, E negative controls and G a shortcut audit. **Measured in repo:** coordinate/half-pixel audits, the severity counterexample, invariant tests, alias diagnostics and the confirmed scorer disagreement support this direction; most full numerical replications need missing manifests or regeneration. **Unverified — venue fit:** metrology/microlithography proceedings or a vision evaluation workshop; a stronger cross-generator and independent-SEM package could fit JM3 or IEEE Transactions on Semiconductor Manufacturing. Outline only: target definitions → audit invariants → controlled failure cases → independent ranking/uncertainty tests → release manifest and limits.

**Unverified — rank 2: Scan-model-aware pose estimation for periodic SEM navigation.** Proposed claim: rigid photometric objectives convert horizontal scan drift into apparent rotation, while an anisotropy-aware residual estimator reduces that bias cheaply. B is primary, C defines targets, A separates pose from repeat errors, and D controls full-frame information. **Measured in repo:** implementation, mechanism tests and documented held-out rotation gains exist. **Unverified:** superiority to affine/ECC/scan-distortion competitors and transfer to another scan model remain open. Venue class: metrology methods, microscopy/instrumentation methods or SPIE proceedings. Outline only: scan model → bias derivation → estimator/degeneracy → joint-affine controls → independent microscopy validation.

**Unverified — rank 3: When richer matching representations fail on synthetic CAD-to-SEM registration.** Proposed claim: identify where intensity, edges, rank, conversion and peak arbitration succeed or fail under matched operating points and controlled forward models. E is primary, F supplies established confidence controls, and C defines validity. **Measured in repo:** threshold-held-out arms and rejected variants are implemented. **Unverified:** a single renderer and reused splits cannot support a broad negative result, especially against successful prior conversion methods. Venue class: narrowly scoped workshop, industrial metrology proceedings or an empirical/negative-results track. Outline only: representation assumptions → calibrated arms → contrast/noise/renderer factorial → paired errors and equivalence tests → boundary conditions.

**Unverified — recommendation:** pursue framing 1 first; keep framing 2 if the joint-affine and independent-scan controls survive. Do not draft a standalone localiser-accuracy, peak-confidence or CAD-anchoring paper from the present record. Venue fit is a judgment, not an acceptance prediction.

## 6. Required experiments, ordered by value per cost

**Unverified — proposed protocol, not completed work:** sample sizes are planning targets and analytic approximations, not guarantees of publication. The leading framing does not require heavy training.

**Unverified — common design:** freeze commit, weight digest, generator/renderer version, target coordinates, input regime, threshold and scorer before confirmation. Split by independent canvas/layout and acquisition session; keep every crop and severity rendering of a source together. Pair by stable IDs/hashes, require the full input roster, reject duplicates and count missing outputs as failures. Use 20,000 paired bootstrap replicates over independent canvas/session clusters, stratified by architecture and presence; recompute F1, AUC, conditional pose denominators and totals inside every replicate. Fit thresholds outside confirmation; refitting requires nested resampling or a separate calibration set. Report raw signed errors, exact-site and alias-aware components, denominators, failures and runtime. Prespecify one primary comparison or correct multiple co-primary tests; do not inspect a holdout repeatedly until significance appears.
